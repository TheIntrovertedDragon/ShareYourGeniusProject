Welcome to my unofficial Wings of Fire Guide!

Here, I will introduce, my favorite book series:
Wings of Fire

Wings of Fire is a children's series written by Tui T. Sutherland about a world of dragons. Currently, there are four arcs in the main series, with the fourth one still being written. Each arc centers around a different main problem, and each book in the arcs (generally five) features a different protagonist.

Themes and Inclusivity

Wings of Fire embraces many themes, even as a children's series. Themes include:
Found family, peace vs. war, empathy, accepting differences, self-worth, morality, perception, and identity

Wings of Fire is also extremely inclusive. Combatting their version of racism is an important theme throughout the series, while more subtle elements of inclusivity are also added. For example, the dragon world is mostly run by female rulers, with lots of female characters being strong and confident. There is also LGBTQIA+ and neurodivergent/disability representation, along with parallels between having diverse cultures in Wings of Fire as compared to the real world.

In general, if you're looking for a high-quality children's series, then check out Wings of Fire!
